Definitions and limitations

Other political committees include multiple committee types. This does not isolate corporate PACs or lobbyist money. A zero does not certify refusal of lobbyist donations. Campaign receipts can include loans and transfers; the categories do not sum to receipts. Raw totals can include transfers between authorized committees.

Campaigns have different reporting schedules. Compare periods before comparing amounts. Records do not prove that money caused a policy decision. This is an offline snapshot; follow the FEC links to check updates.
